Skin Care Foods

Glow from within

Avocado — Rich in Vitamin E & healthy fats. Hydrates skin, reduces inflammation.
Blueberries — Packed with antioxidants that fight free radicals and slow aging.
Sweet Potato — Beta-carotene converts to Vitamin A for skin cell renewal.
Green Tea — Catechins reduce skin damage from UV rays.
Turmeric — Curcumin has potent anti-inflammatory and brightening properties.
⚡

Energy Boosting

Natural vitality

Bananas — Quick carbs + B6 for sustained energy without the crash.
Oats — Complex carbohydrates for stable blood sugar and 4+ hrs energy.
Almonds — Magnesium & B vitamins convert food into cellular energy.
Chia Seeds — Omega-3s, fiber, and protein for marathon-level endurance.
Lentils — Iron + folate reduce fatigue, increase oxygen transport.
💪

Weight Management

Healthy transformation

Broccoli — High fiber, very low calorie. Fills you up and supports metabolism.
Greek Yogurt — Protein + probiotics boost metabolism and gut health.
Apple Cider Vinegar — Reduces appetite and improves insulin sensitivity.
Cayenne Pepper — Capsaicin increases thermogenesis by up to 5%.
Green Tea — EGCG catechins measurably boost fat burning.

Nutrient Verification

Full macronutrient, micronutrient, and phytonutrient analysis using HPLC and mass spectrometry for lab-accurate vitamin and mineral readings.

☣️

Contaminant Testing

Pesticide residue, heavy metal (lead, mercury, arsenic, cadmium), mycotoxin, and bacterial contamination screening on every batch.

Chemical-Free Certification
